Privacy Statement for the LightField Reporting Service

Last updated: March 2019

The LightField Reporting Service is designed to collect information about problems and errors that affect the use and performance of Teledyne Princeton Instruments software in order to diagnose and correct problems with the software and provide better functionality. Teledyne Princeton Instruments is committed to helping protect your privacy. This statement explains how the LightField Reporting service collects information and how the information can be used. This statement does not apply to other online or offline Teledyne Princeton Instruments websites, software, or services.

What types of information can be collected?

The LightField Reporting service can collect information about problems that interrupt you while you work and information about errors that occur behind the scenes. It's important to diagnose these types of errors because these problems, if left unsolved, might cause additional problems such as performance or program failures. Reports contain information useful for diagnosing and solving problems that have occurred, such as:

  • Files that help describe the problem. These failures are typically system or report-generated files about software behavior before or after the problem occurred.
  • Basic software and hardware information. This information can include details about your Teledyne Princeton Instruments hardware.

Reports generated by the reporting service might unintentionally contain personal information. For example, a report that contains a screenshot might include your name, part of a document you were working on, or other information that was visible on your screen at the time of the crash. No personal or personally identifiable information will be used by Teledyne Princeton Instruments to identify or contact you or be sold to third parties and, except as described in this statement, we will not disclose your personal information outside of Teledyne Princeton Instruments and its affiliates without your consent.

Why does Teledyne Princeton Instruments collect information about errors and problems?

Information collected through the Reporting Service is used by Teledyne Princeton Instruments to diagnose problems in the software you use and to provide solutions. Not all problems have solutions but when solutions are available, Teledyne Princeton Instruments may offer updates to install or steps for solving a problem you've reported. To help prevent problems and make software more reliable, some solutions are also included in future versions of the software.

How is information collected?

If a problem occurs in LightField, you may be asked if you want to report it. You can view the details of the report before sending it, although some files might not be in a readable format. When you send a report the gathered information is placed in an e-mail and sent to the software department in our Trenton offices. After you send a report, Teledyne Princeton Instruments may contact you to request additional information to help solve the problem you reported. Reporting is purely voluntary: if you are concerned that a report may contain personal or confidential information, you should not send the report.

Who can use the information and how can it be used?

Teledyne Princeton Instruments uses information collected by the reporting service to improve Teledyne Princeton Instruments products and services as well as third-party software and hardware designed for use with these products and services. Teledyne Princeton Instruments employees, contractors, vendors, and partners may be provided access to information about specific errors and problems on a confidential basis; however, they are only authorized to use the information to repair or improve Teledyne Princeton Instruments products and services and third-party software and hardware designed for use with Teledyne Princeton Instruments products and services. For example, if a report indicates that a third-party product is involved, Teledyne Princeton Instruments may send that information to the vendor of the product. The vendor may provide the information to sub- vendors and partners; however, all parties must abide by the terms of this privacy statement.

Information storage, processing, and release

Information collected by the LightField Reporting service may be stored and processed in the United States or in other countries in which Teledyne Princeton Instruments or its affiliates, service providers, or partners maintain facilities. Teledyne Princeton Instruments abides by the safe harbor frameworks as set forth by the U.S. Department of Commerce regarding the collection, use, and retention of data from the European Union, the European Economic Area, and Switzerland. Additional information regarding the Safe Harbor program is available at http://www.export.gov/safeharbor. Teledyne Princeton Instruments may access or disclose information about you, including the content of your communications, in order to:

  1. Comply with the law or respond to lawful requests or legal process.
  2. Protect the rights or property of Teledyne Princeton Instruments or its affiliates, agents, partners, or customers.
  3. Act on a good faith belief that such access or disclosure is necessary to protect the personal safety of Teledyne Princeton Instruments, its employees, affiliates, agents, partners, or customers, or the public.

Security Practices

Teledyne Princeton Instruments is committed to helping protect the security of your information. We use a variety of security technologies and procedures to help protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. For example, we store the information you provide on computer systems with limited access, which are located in controlled facilities.

Changes to this privacy statement

We will occasionally update this privacy statement to reflect changes in our products, services, procedures, and customer feedback. When we post changes, we will revise the "last updated" date at the top of this statement. We encourage you to periodically review this statement to be informed of how Teledyne Princeton Instruments is protecting your information.
